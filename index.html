Production Schedule

Planning estimates for Furnace A and Furnace B, including billet waiting and rolling.

Planning model: 210 minutes per melting/casting cycle; Furnace B begins 60 minutes after A; each furnace repeats every 210 minutes. Casting and CCM cooling times are not independently confirmed.

Important: The calculated CCM event is a planning marker at cycle end, not a verified casting completion time. If an actual casting end is entered, the 150-minute wait is measured from that time for the first A heat only. Other rows remain theoretical. No records are written to the database by this calculator.

Staff accounts & roles

Each staff member signs in with a separate email and password. Step 1: In Supabase Dashboard, open Authentication → Users → Add user and create an individual sign-in for each employee. Step 2: Return here, press Refresh, select the employee's job role and Save role. Only then can they access their department.

A staff account starts as Unassigned (read only). Assign Melting, Billet Transfer, Rolling, Sales, or Loading for department access. Staff cannot edit or delete saved records without Admin approval. Never share account passwords.
