# ADR-003 — Computed inventory before physical stock ledger
**Status:** Accepted for student prototype, not final warehouse management | **Date:** 2026-10-29

**Context:** Need to avoid two sources of truth for finished tonnage.

**Decision:** Compute inventory from completed rolling batches minus eligible loaded/dispatched events per bar size; prohibit over-dispatch inside a transaction.

**Alternatives:** mutable stock quantity table, journal/ledger with immutable inventory events.

**Consequences:** No balance drift from forgetting to update a separate table. Weakness: direct admin corrections/deletions can retroactively change stock; lot tracking, returns, reservations and multi-size split are unsupported. A proper immutable ledger is recommended before plant-wide rollout.
